# The Gilliverse: Breaking Bad & Better Call Saul

> [!NOTE]
> Both series are set in Albuquerque, New Mexico, and were created by Vince Gilligan (with Peter Gould co-creating *Better Call Saul*). Together, they form a unified crime drama universe spanning 11 seasons and a feature film.

## Series Overview

**Breaking Bad** (2008–2013) follows Walter White, a mild-mannered high school chemistry teacher diagnosed with terminal lung cancer. To secure his family's financial future, he partners with former student Jesse Pinkman to manufacture and distribute crystal methamphetamine, descending into a ruthless drug kingpin known as *Heisenberg*.

**Better Call Saul** (2015–2022) serves primarily as a prequel (with flash-forwards to a post-*Breaking Bad* timeline). It chronicles the moral decay of Jimmy McGill, an earnest but struggling con-artist-turned-lawyer who gradually transforms into the flamboyant, morally bankrupt criminal defense attorney **Saul Goodman**.

## Key Characters & Transformations

* **Walter White** $\rightarrow$ ~~Mr. White~~ $\rightarrow$ **Heisenberg**
* **Jimmy McGill** $\rightarrow$ *Slippin' Jimmy* $\rightarrow$ **Saul Goodman** $\rightarrow$ `Gene Takavic`
* **Kim Wexler**: A sharp, ambitious attorney whose loyalty to Jimmy pulls her into increasingly destructive schemes.
* **Mike Ehrmantraut**: A former Philadelphia cop working as a parking attendant, private investigator, and eventual cartel fixer.
* **Gus Fring**: The calculated, soft-spoken proprietor of *Los Pollos Hermanos* who operates a massive methamphetamine empire beneath a facade of philanthropy.
